Animals

Animals are living, breathing creatures that share our planet and play a vital role in nature. They are generally divided into two main groups: domestic animals and wild animals. Domestic animals—such as dogs, cats, cows, and horses—live closely with humans, offering companionship, security, and help with farm work or transport. Wild animals—such as lions, tigers, and elephants—live freely in forests, jungles, and grasslands, maintaining the natural balance of the ecosystem. Protecting all animals is essential because human survival and well-being are deeply connected to the natural world.
